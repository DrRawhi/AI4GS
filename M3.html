Activity 1

AI Learning Type

Classify practical examples and explain the clues behind your choices.

10–15 minutes
Objective: Understand the difference between Supervised Learning and Unsupervised Learning.
Background: Supervised Learning uses historical examples with known answers to predict future outcomes. Unsupervised Learning does not know the correct answers; it discovers hidden patterns, relationships, or natural groups.
