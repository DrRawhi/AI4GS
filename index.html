Stage 1 Details

Description:

This Stage 1: Foundations of Agentic AI & Custom GPTs for Government Services provides a practical introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Agentic AI, demonstrating how these technologies can transform government services. Through interactive demonstrations and hands-on activities, participants will learn to identify AI opportunities across the citizen journey, apply responsible AI principles, and use prompt engineering to design and build a Custom GPT tailored to a real government service. By the end of this stage, each participant will have developed a working Custom GPT that can support communication, document analysis, policy interpretation, and internal government workflows. Stage 1 serves as the foundation for Stage 2: Data-Driven Government Decision Support & Intelligent AI Agents, where participants will build on these skills to develop intelligent AI agents and data-driven decision support solutions for government services.
