Stage 2 Details

AI4ALL – AI for Government Services (AI4GS) is a 10-hour hands-on training program that helps government and administrative staff use AI to improve productivity, decision-making, and service delivery. Across four practical modules, participants explore AI foundations, build customized AI assistants, use Orange Data Mining to analyze and visualize data, and design intelligent AI agents that integrate organizational knowledge and multi-step workflows. The program emphasizes practical government applications, responsible AI use, data privacy, and human oversight.

Overall Curriculum Structure

  • Stage 1: Foundations of Agentic AI & Custom GPTs for Government Services
    • Module 1: AI & Agentic AI Foundations for Government Services
    • Module 2: Prompt Engineering & Building Custom GPT Plugins
  • Stage 2: Data-Driven Government Decision Support & Intelligent AI Agents
    • Module 3: Data-Driven Decision Support
    • Module 4: Planning Intelligent AI Agents
