Stage 2 Details

AI4ALL – Stage 2: Data-Driven Decision Support & Intelligent AI Agents is a 5-hour hands-on program designed to help participants use data and AI to support smarter government decision-making and service delivery. The program covers Data-Driven Decision Support, where participants use Orange Data Mining to explore and visualize data, apply classification and clustering techniques, and generate actionable insights, followed by Building Intelligent AI Agents, where participants transform a Custom GPT into an Agentic AI Assistant by integrating organizational knowledge, designing multi-step workflows, automating routine tasks, and supporting decision-making through practical government use cases. By the end of the program, participants will gain practical experience in combining data analytics, AI, and Agentic AI workflows to develop a working intelligent assistant for government operations, decision support, and service delivery.
