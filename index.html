Stage 1 Details

Description:

AI for Government Services (AI4GS) is a practical training initiative designed to equip government professionals with the knowledge and hands-on skills needed to apply Artificial Intelligence (AI) effectively in government services. The program combines AI and Agentic AI concepts, data-driven decision-making, prompt engineering, Custom GPTs, and intelligent AI agents through practical activities and real-world government scenarios. AI4GS aims to help participants identify opportunities for AI adoption, analyze data for evidence-based insights, design intelligent solutions, and improve the efficiency, quality, and delivery of government services.

Overall Curriculum Structure

  • Stage 1: Foundations of Agentic AI & Custom GPTs for Government Services
    • Module 1: AI & Agentic AI Foundations for Government Services
    • Module 2: Prompt Engineering & Building Custom GPTs
  • Stage 2: Data-Driven Government Decision Support & Intelligent AI Agents
    • Module 3: Data-Driven Decision Support
    • Module 4: Building Intelligent AI Agents
