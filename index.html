Training Information

About the Training:
Gain a practical introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Agentic AI and discover how these technologies can transform government services. Through interactive demonstrations and hands-on activities, participants will learn to identify AI opportunities across the citizen journey, apply responsible AI principles, and use prompt engineering to design and build a Custom GPT tailored to a real government service. By the end of the stage, each participant will have developed a working Custom GPT that can support communication, document analysis, policy interpretation, and internal government workflows.
